Kerala Railway

"Discover the beauty of Kerala through the comfort and convenience of rail travel. Kerala Railways connects vibrant cities, lush backwaters, serene beaches, and misty hill stations, bringing every corner of God's Own Country within reach. Whether you're commuting, exploring, or embarking on a new adventure, Kerala Railways offers a seamless and scenic journey through landscapes that capture the spirit of Kerala. Sit back, relax, and let us take you on a memorable ride across one of India’s most enchanting states."

SouthernKerala Railway

Serving as the main artery for Kerala’s southern regions, Southern Kerala Railway connects bustling urban centers with serene coastal and backwater destinations, making travel seamless and scenic.

Western Kerala Railway

Running along Kerala’s picturesque western coast, Western Kerala Railway offers a unique blend of coastal and cultural journeys. From tranquil beaches to historic towns

Southwest Kerala Railway

Southwest Kerala Railway seamlessly links the southern coast with the state’s western regions, allowing passengers to explore Kerala’s rich diversity. Ideal for those traveling between urban hubs and coastal town

Northeast Kerala Railway

Traversing Kerala’s northeast, this line opens up access to the state’s mountainous and lush landscapes, reaching districts like Wayanad and Malappuram.
